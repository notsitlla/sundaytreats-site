Our story

We bake small.
We don't cut corners.

Sunday Treats is a neighborhood cottage bakery for people who love a really good cookie and the feeling of giving one away.

Every batch is made from simple, quality ingredients, baked fresh, and wrapped with the same care whether it is a gift for one or a box for a whole team.

  • Small-batch baking — never mass-produced
  • Thoughtful presentation for gifting and events
  • Proudly serving Littleton, Highlands Ranch, and Denver
